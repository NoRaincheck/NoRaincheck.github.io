## The Comic Book Font

_March 2025_

One random thing I learnt is that the comic book font that you see in hand-drawn
style comics is actually typically done via technical lettering tools like
[Leroy Lettering System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_lettering).
I've been thinking of improving my handwriting and quite enjoy that "Gothic
Router" aesthetic (which is used in this
[blog](https://github.com/HeardACat/nationalpark-webfont/)) due to its
"draft-like" look, and unrefined appearance which emphasises on the informal
nature of my writing. To that end I came across this lettering guide which I
will probably lean on in the future.

_edit: April 2025, the font is now in Google Fonts_

I think the only one which looks dated is the approach to the ampersand. From my
perspective it would be better to do a backslash followed by the lettering of
the number 8. In the future maybe I'll modernise the above and animate it using
SVG.
